Dawson Landscaping & Maintenance works with Perth homeowners to build outdoor spaces that look good on day one and still look good years later.

Our story

Dawson Landscaping & Maintenance is a locally owned Perth business offering landscaping, garden renovations and ongoing maintenance for residential properties.

The approach is simple: understand how you want to use your outdoor space, plan the work honestly, prepare the site properly and finish it cleanly. Whether that's a full backyard rebuild or a fortnightly maintenance visit, the standard is the same.

We work across the Perth metropolitan area and are happy to talk through options and rough budgets before you commit to anything.
